# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient with others
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as an address or email, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at randeshdaluwatte@gmail.com. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they find harmful.
